Mortgage & Affordability Calculator

UAE Central Bank LTV caps and the 50% debt-burden ratio, applied to find the maximum loan, the binding constraint and the price your buyer can actually afford.

Buyer profile

Off-plan purchases are capped at 50% LTV regardless of buyer type or property count — this overrides the tiers above.

CBUAE caps: UAE National first home 85% (≤ AED 5M) / 75% above; Expat resident first home 80% (≤ AED 5M) / 70% above; additional property 60%; non-resident 50%. Total monthly debt — new mortgage payment plus existing obligations — must stay within 50% of gross monthly income.

Finances

Car loans, personal loans, credit-card minimums — anything the bank already counts in the debt-burden ratio.

Loan

Maximum loan is the lower of the LTV cap on the price and the loan the income can service at this rate and term.

Bank & registration costs

Mortgage registration is 0.25% of the loan amount plus AED 290, payable to the Dubai Land Department. Lenders also require life insurance (mortgage protection), typically ~0.4–0.5% per year of the outstanding balance — often quoted as a monthly premium on top of the payment.
