AED is pegged to the US dollar at 3.6725. FX series are yearly averages (sample data). Rent is converted at each year's rate, and the return figures sum those per-year converted amounts — so the table and the headline reconcile.

The final year is a partial year: its FX rate is the year-to-date average and a full year of rent may not yet have been collected.
