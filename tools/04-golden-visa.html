Rules per DLD/GDRFA: the AED 2,000,000 threshold is on purchase price per title deed (ready) or Oqood (off-plan); multiple properties may combine. Mortgaged properties have been eligible since January 2024 — the old AED 1M minimum cash-paid rule was removed — but a bank NOC showing the amount already paid is required. Off-plan properties count at full price only when at least 50% of the price is paid and construction is at least 50% complete. Verify current requirements with GDRFA before filing.
