as of Feb 2026 Rules per DLD/GDRFA: the AED 2,000,000 threshold is on purchase price per title deed (ready) or Oqood (off-plan); multiple properties may combine. Mortgaged properties have been eligible since January 2024 — the old AED 1M minimum cash-paid rule was removed — but a bank NOC showing the amount already paid is required. Since February 2026, off-plan properties count at their full DLD-certified (Oqood) value regardless of the amount paid or construction stage, provided the property is from a RERA/DLD-approved developer and Oqood-registered. Joint owners: each applicant's share must independently reach AED 2,000,000. Verify current requirements with GDRFA before filing.
