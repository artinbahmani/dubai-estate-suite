Off-plan, resale and rental commission with 5% VAT and agent/brokerage splits.

Off-plan deal

Buyer pays 0% — the developer pays the brokerage 3–7% of the unit price. Off-plan is typically 2–3x more profitable per deal than resale.

Resale deal

Dual agency is legal in Dubai: the brokerage collects 2% from the buyer and 2% from the seller, so the fee is doubled.

2% of the sale price is customary on the secondary market, paid by the buyer (tenant pays on rentals).

Rental deal

Commission is 5% of the annual rent, subject to an AED 5,000 minimum, paid by the tenant.

Split & VAT

VAT of 5% is charged on top of the commission and remitted to the FTA — it is not split between agent and brokerage. Splits of 50/50 to 80/20 are typical; higher producers negotiate up.
